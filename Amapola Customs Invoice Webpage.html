5 · Wood packaging (ISPM 15) — pallets / wooden boxes

You do not treat the wood — this issues a formal company declaration that the shipment is packed on a pallet bearing a valid IPPC mark, verified from the stamp. Read the country code & registration number off the stamp; attaching a photo (Annex A) makes it far more credible at Brazilian customs.
